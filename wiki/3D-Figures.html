3D figures

PIRA has posable wooden mannequins you can draw over, inside the layer stack. A 3D layer behaves like any other layer: opacity, blend modes, masks and clipping all apply, so you can fade the figure and sketch on a layer above it.

Adding a figure

  • Layer › New › 3D Figure…, or
  • open the 3D panel and tap a pose: it goes onto the selected figure, or makes a new 3D layer when the current layer isn't one.

The 3D panel can add a male or female figure, put more than one figure on a layer, and remove them.

Posing with the Object tool

Pick the Object tool (with the Move tool in the toolbar), then:

  • Drag a body part to swing it. Turn on Twist (or hold Alt) to turn it about its own bone.
  • Drag the hips to slide the whole figure; with Twist, to turn it.
  • Joints mode: drag the head, elbows, wrists, knees and ankles to where they should be, and the limbs follow.
  • Drag empty canvas to orbit the camera; Shift pans and Ctrl moves closer or further.

The options bar has the drag modes, camera views, fit, place on floor, mirror and flip, and menus for the figure, its look and the light. Layer › Rasterize › 3D Layer turns it into plain pixels.

The pose library

The 3D panel lists poses by category, with a search:

  • 11 built-in poses, and
  • 1,072 motion-capture poses from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database.
  • Hand poses for the left, right or both hands.

Tap a pose to apply it, then fine-tune any joint with the Object tool.

Look

The look menu sets shaded, toon, flat or silhouette rendering, an outline's width and colour, and the floor shadow.

Lighting

Lighting is in the 3D Light panel:

  • Drag the sun: in Light mode, drag on the disc around the figure. The centre lights from the camera, the rim from the side, and out to the outer ring from behind, for rim light.
  • Presets: upper left or right, front, sides, overhead, below, rim.
  • Sliders for direction, height, strength and ambient light, and a light colour.
  • Turns with the camera (the default) or stays fixed in the scene as you orbit.
  • Second light: an optional fill or rim light with its own direction, strength and colour. With the floor shadow on, it casts its own lighter shadow. Its smaller sun, marked 2, can be dragged too.

Gravity

The 3D panel's Gravity tab drops the figure so it falls and hangs with real weight and joint limits:

  1. Pick the Object tool's Pin mode and tap the parts to hold still: pin a hand to hang the figure from it, or the feet to keep them planted. Remove All Pins clears them.
  2. Set a Push (which way and how hard) and Muscle tone (how limp or stiff).
  3. Tap Drop. Play or scrub through the fall over the canvas.
  4. Keep the moment you want (one undo step), or Cancel. Drop again tries another fall.

Match a pose to a photo

Put a photo under the 3D layer and PIRA poses the figure like the person in it. See Match a pose to a photo.

Saving

.pcraft saves the whole scene, so you can come back and re-pose it. PSD saves the figure as pixels.

Credits

Motion-capture poses come from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, mocap.cs.cmu.edu, created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217. They are not to be sold as a separate pack.
