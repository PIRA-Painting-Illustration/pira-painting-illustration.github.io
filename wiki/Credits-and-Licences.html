Credits and licences

Help › About › Credits in the app lists everyone and everything PIRA is built on. In short:

  • PhotoCraft by the ArtCraft contributors: PIRA's engine and much of its interface, under the MIT or Apache 2.0 licence. PIRA is an independent project, not made or supported by PhotoCraft or ArtCraft.
  • Motion-capture poses: the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, mocap.cs.cmu.edu, created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217. The poses come with PIRA and PIRA Pose and are not to be sold as a separate pack.
  • Pose tracking: Google's MediaPipe models (Apache 2.0), from the OpenCV Zoo.
  • Fonts: Inter and JetBrains Mono for the interface, and Philosopher for PIRA's titles, all under the SIL Open Font License.

The full list, with licence texts, is in ATTRIBUTION.md and NOTICE in the repository and in the desktop downloads.
