Comic pages

A page ready for print

In File › New, the Comic presets set up manga B5, US comic, A4, square and webtoon pages. The canvas is the trim size plus the bleed, and guides mark:

  • the trim line, where the printed page is cut, and
  • the safe area inside it, where text and faces stay clear of the cut.

Panels

  • Layer › Comic › New Panels lays a grid of panels over the safe area. Each panel is a group masked to its outline, with an art layer inside and an ink border.
  • The Panel Cut tool (K, grouped with Frame) splits a panel: drag a line across it and it divides with a gutter between the halves. Its options bar sets the gutter width and has New Panels and the other comic buttons.
  • Draw inside a panel's art layer and the panel's mask keeps the art inside the frame.

Balloons

Layer › Comic › New Balloon adds a speech balloon with a tail and the words centred in it. Shapes: round, rectangle, cloud or burst. The words are an ordinary text layer, so you can edit and style them.

Effect lines

Effect Lines (on the Panel Cut tool's options bar) draws focus lines around a selection or speed lines across it, as shape layers you can still edit.

Screentone

Layer › Comic › Screentone adds a halftone dot tone masked to the selection, with a line frequency (lpi), density and the usual 45° angle. It's a Pattern Fill layer, so you can repaint its mask to change where the tone shows.

Tips

  • Put the inks of all panels on reference layers and flat each panel with the Paint Bucket.
  • Use special rulers for speed lines and perspective backgrounds.
  • 3D figures make quick reference for poses and camera angles in a panel.
