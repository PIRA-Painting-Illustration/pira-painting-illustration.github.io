Troubleshooting and FAQ

Is PIRA free?

Yes. No subscription, no account, no ads, and no features locked behind a paid tier. It works offline: you never need to log in or connect to draw, save or export.

Is it open source?

PIRA is built on PhotoCraft, which is open source under the MIT or Apache 2.0 licence.

Which devices does it run on?

Android tablets (64-bit ARM), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), and Linux (x86_64). There's no Mac build. PIRA is tested on Android tablets first.

My strokes ignore pen pressure

  1. Check Pressure for Size and Pressure for Opacity in the options bar.
  2. Try another brush: some brushes are set to ignore pressure.
  3. On Android, open Window › Touch Input › Touch Input Settings… and press the pen on the screen: the pen should be reported as a pen, not a finger.
  4. On Windows, make sure the tablet's driver is installed; PIRA reads pens through Windows' own pen support and through WinTab.

My palm draws on the canvas

Palm rejection ignores fingers while the pen is down or near the screen. If a palm still draws, set what one finger does to Move or Nothing in Window › Touch Input.

The fill leaks into the next area

Raise Close Gap on the Paint Bucket, or close the gap in the line. See Line art and colouring.

A menu item is greyed out

It needs something that isn't there: an open canvas, a selection, a particular kind of layer. Export Timelapse needs a recording; it tells you what's missing when you choose it.

Windows says the publisher is unknown

PIRA isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info › Run anyway.

Where are my settings?

SystemFolder
AndroidThe app's own storage (removed if you uninstall)
Windows%APPDATA%\PIRA

Reporting a problem

Open an issue in PIRA's issue tracker, or use Help › Report an Issue… in the app. Say which device and version you're on (Help › About), and what you did just before the problem.
