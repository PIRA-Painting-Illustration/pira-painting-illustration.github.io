A painting app for
pen tablets.

PIRA is a free drawing and painting app for pen tablets. It runs on Android tablets first, and on Windows and Linux too. Pencils and brushes respond to pressure and tilt as soon as you install it, and it works offline with no account.

A 3D mannequin in your painting app

Pose a figure, light it and sketch over it without leaving the canvas. The video and screenshots are from the app running on an Android tablet.

PIRA Pose: the mannequin on its own

Only need a reference figure? PIRA Pose is PIRA's 3D mannequin as a free app of its own, for Android tablets, Windows and Linux. It shares PIRA's posing code, so both apps pose, light and fall the same way.

It has the same motion-capture pose library and hand poses, photo matching, gravity with pins, two lights and up to 64 figures. Export PNGs with a transparent background or over your photo, and save scenes to come back to later.

What comes first

We are working on the pencil stroke, the canvas and layered files first. The long list of extra features other editors have can wait.

01 · Works offline

You don't need to log in, activate a licence or connect to the internet to draw, save or export.

02 · Made for the pen

Brushes use pen pressure and tilt from the start, and the touch gestures were tested on real tablets.

03 · Open source

Anyone can read the code. The file and brush formats are documented, and you can import your own brushes.

Roadmap

Where PIRA is now and what comes next. Plans may change after testing and feedback.

Done1 · Painting core

Pencils, inks and brushes with pressure and tilt, layers, selections and layered files.

Done2 · 3D figures

Posable mannequins, over a thousand poses, photo matching, studio lighting and gravity.

Now3 · Public beta

The code is public and the beta is out for Android, Windows and Linux, with updates delivered in the app.

Later4 · Animation

Frame-by-frame animation on the canvas: onion skin, a timeline made for the pen, and export to video and GIF.

Status

PIRA is an independent project, unrelated to ArtCraft. It is built on PhotoCraft, which is open source under the MIT or Apache 2.0 licence.
